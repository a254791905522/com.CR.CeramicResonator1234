How the workshop plays

Ceramic Resonator is a physical-simulation puzzle: the terracotta actually curves and settles under each resonance, rendered with detailed surface shaders.

Physics

The clay answers every tap

Watch the surface deform as waves travel. Rough texture, burnt-orange light, and drifting studio dust keep the bench feeling lived-in rather than abstract.

Combinations

Tabs, cancellations, falloff

Start with a single vibration. Later profiles ask you to cancel waves against each other and judge how strength fades with distance.

Twenty profiles

Three phases, one quiet rule

Levels are handcrafted and grow more tactical, yet nothing rushes you. There is no countdown and no instant fail for trying a bold pattern. Take the time the clay needs.

Stars

Score the firing, not the clock

Plan the shortest sequence that reproduces the target. Clean solutions earn three stars; messy ones still teach the next profile.
